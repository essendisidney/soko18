# Privacy

People want control over who sees them. These are features, not secrecy tools.

## Incognito (KES 299 / month, included in Platinum)

- Only people you've liked can see you on Discover and Browse
- Enforced in the database (`profile_hidden_from_me`), not just the UI

## Contact block (hashed)

- Type a number or pick contacts. We store **peppered SHA-256 hashes only**, never raw numbers
- "People in your contacts won't see you here"

## Names

- First name or nickname. No surname or employer required

## Chats

- Read receipts, report, block. Messages about rates or paid meetups are held for review

## Waitlists

- Real counts only. Empty stays empty. Friend pass speeds review; it never mints credit

## Safety (free)

- Panic and live location go only to your trusted contacts
- Report hides them from your Discover straight away. Three unique reports → staff review and hidden from the public feed
